1. Overview

PDFcue is a PDF reading, editing, scanning, form-filling, OCR, and document-management application for Android. PDFcue is designed to carry out document operations locally on your device. This Privacy Policy explains what data PDFcue can access and store, what external services are involved, and how you can manage your data.

PDFcue does not operate a document-upload server for its normal PDF tools. PDFs, scanned pages, extracted document text, saved signatures, and information entered into My Details are not uploaded to a PDFcue-operated server for processing. Certain Google services used for OCR/scanning components and subscriptions may separately process limited technical or transaction-related information, as explained below.

2. Information you choose to provide or allow the app to access

When you use PDFcue, the app may access or process:

  • Documents and their content: PDFs and other supported documents you open, edit, merge, compare, organize, convert, print, export, or share. They may contain names, addresses, financial information, signatures, or other sensitive material that you put in the file.
  • Photos and scanned images: Images you select or capture to create PDFs or insert into documents.
  • Form and profile details: Information you choose to save in My Details, such as names, contact details, addresses, and custom form values, for reuse in forms and Smart Fill/AutoFill.
  • Signatures: Signature images and labels that you explicitly save for reuse.
  • Local usage records: Recent-file references, bookmarks, reading positions, selected settings, and local processing status needed for app features.
  • Purchase status: Information returned through Google Play Billing, such as whether a PDFcue Pro subscription is active, to determine access to paid features. PDFcue does not ask for or store your full payment-card details.

PDFcue does not require you to create a PDFcue account to use its local document tools.

3. How information is used and processed

PDFcue uses document and profile information only to provide the features you request, including opening and editing files, recognizing text on scanned pages, filling forms, applying signatures, making exports, and restoring interrupted editing sessions.

PDF rendering, editing, conversion, and OCR of document content are designed to run locally. Google ML Kit's document scanning and text recognition process image and text input on-device rather than sending that input or its recognized text to Google servers. Google services may nevertheless process technical diagnostic information, as described in Section 5.

PDFcue does not use your document content for advertising, does not sell it, and does not include an advertising SDK or an app-operated cloud analytics service in the reviewed version.

4. Device permissions and user-selected files

PDFcue primarily uses Android's system document picker / Storage Access Framework and Photo Picker, which let you choose particular files or images. For scanning, PDFcue uses the Google ML Kit document scanner where available or can hand off capture to a device camera application.

Some distribution variants may offer an optional Scan device feature that requests Android's special All files access permission. If present and granted, that permission is used to locate and work with local PDF documents. It is not a permission to upload your files. You can revoke it in Android Settings. The standard Play distribution is intended to use selected-file access instead of All files access.

PDFcue may use Android's foreground-service facilities for longer document-processing tasks. Permission availability varies by Android version and distribution variant.

5. Google services and other third parties

PDFcue uses the following third-party services when their related features are available:

  • Google ML Kit and Google Play services — OCR / document scanning: Document images and recognized text are processed on the device. According to Google's ML Kit disclosures, SDKs may collect or transmit technical data such as device and app information, feature-use events, performance metrics, error codes, and certain installation or device identifiers for diagnostics, usage analysis, service maintenance, and model updates. Google Play services may download scanner components or models. See Google's ML Kit privacy information, ML Kit Android data disclosures, and Google Privacy Policy.
  • Google Play Billing — PDFcue Pro: Google processes subscriptions and payments under its own policies. PDFcue receives purchase or subscription status and related transaction identifiers needed to provide or restore Pro access. Payment-card details are handled by Google, not stored by PDFcue. See the Google Privacy Policy.
  • Other apps or storage providers you choose: If you open a document from a cloud-storage provider, save or share a file to another application, or print using Android's print framework, that selected provider or app may process the file under its own privacy practices. Such sharing occurs at your direction.

PDFcue does not sell personal information or share document contents with advertising networks. This does not prevent the above service providers from handling the technical or billing data needed for their services.

6. Local storage, security, and backups

PDFcue stores working files, selected app data, and caches in app-private device storage as needed to operate. My Details and reusable saved signatures are encrypted using Android Keystore-backed cryptographic keys. Other temporary document files and search indexes are stored in app-private storage; this does not mean that every PDF saved elsewhere on the device is encrypted by PDFcue.

The app's Android backup configuration disables backup of its private app data. Files you explicitly save to shared storage, a document provider, or another application are controlled by the location you select. Keep your device protected and choose trusted destinations when handling sensitive PDFs.

7. Retention and deletion

  • Documents and exports: Copies explicitly saved by you remain at the storage location you selected until you delete them. PDFcue does not delete your exported files when you uninstall the app.
  • Saved profile and signatures: Stored on the device until you edit/delete them through the app, clear the app's data, or uninstall PDFcue. Where available, use My Details clearing and individual saved-signature deletion.
  • Recent files and settings: Held locally for convenience until cleared within the app where supported, or until app data is cleared or the app is uninstalled.
  • Search index: Local cached document text, including OCR results, may be kept to speed up searches. The reviewed app expires index files after approximately 30 days and caps the total index at 64 MB, subject to local cleanup behavior.
  • Interrupted-work recovery: Minimal recovery information and an app-private working copy may be retained for up to approximately 24 hours to support recovery after an interruption. Normal closing and cleanup can delete recovery copies sooner.
  • Other temporary files and background jobs: Retained only as necessary for processing, retries, or cleanup; some app-private caches may remain until Android or the app clears them, or until the app is uninstalled.
  • Google-managed transactions and diagnostics: Google retains relevant billing and technical records according to its own policies and retention schedules.

To remove remaining PDFcue private data from the device, you can use Android Settings → Apps → PDFcue → Storage → Clear data, or uninstall the application. This may erase unsaved work and cannot delete copies stored outside PDFcue. To manage Google Play subscriptions, use Google Play's subscription controls.

8. Your choices

You control which documents and images you select, whether to save profile fields or signatures, whether to purchase Pro, and whether to share or export documents to other services. You may revoke any optional file-access permissions in Android Settings. For questions about local data or a privacy request, contact the developer using the address below.

9. Children's privacy

PDFcue is a general-purpose document utility and is not specifically directed to children. The developer does not intentionally seek children's personal information through a PDFcue account or document-upload service. If you believe personal information has been provided to the developer in error, contact the developer.

10. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when PDFcue's features, SDKs, data practices, or legal requirements change. The Last updated date will be revised when a new version is published. Material changes may also be communicated through the app or its Google Play listing when appropriate.
